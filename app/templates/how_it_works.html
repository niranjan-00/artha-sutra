HOW ARTHA SUTRA WORKS

Keep today visible.
Make tomorrow less hazy.

Artha Sutra connects the money already in your business with receipts and payments you expect next.

01

Start with your real cash

Set an opening balance, choose a minimum reserve and record settled income and expenses. Pending entries stay out of actual cash.

02

Track what’s still owed

Add customer invoices and supplier bills. Record full or partial payments; the outstanding amount and cash movement update together.

03

Review the dated forecast

See daily estimates for the next 7, 30 or 90 days. Overdue customer receipts are not silently treated as guaranteed income.

04

Try a what-if

Test a delayed bill, a moved receipt or a one-off expense without changing your actual transactions or balances.

Forecasts are estimates, not promises.

They use recorded amounts, dates and a transparent daily calculation. Missing or overdue dates are called out rather than filled in with invented certainty.

Start with what you know.

You can add more business details and financial records as you go.
