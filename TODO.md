- [x] Use Decimal in Python and a consistent precise SQL money representation such as integer paise; document rupee/paise conversion. Binary floating-point must not be the authoritative money representation. Handle failed/related database updates atomically.
- [x] Transactions include business ID, type, description, amount, category, date, payment method, payment status, optional customer/supplier, reference/notes, creation/update timestamps. Support income, expense, customer payment, supplier payment, refund and account transfer if supported. Payment methods include cash, UPI, bank transfer, card and other.
- [x] Users can add, view details, edit and delete transactions after confirmation; search; filter by date/type/category/payment status; sort; and export CSV. Server validates amount and date. Edits/deletions update dashboard and forecasts.
- [x] Financial correctness: pending payments are not settled cash; credit sales do not increase cash until received; unpaid supplier bills do not reduce actual cash before payment; each payment affects cash exactly once; account transfers are not income or expense; do not double-count payment categories. Future-dated transactions/payments cannot be marked settled and must remain pending until the cash movement occurs.
- [x] Customer and supplier records store separate name, optional phone/email/address/notes and business association; support add/view/edit/search/filter, related invoices, outstanding balances and payment history. Do not store party data only in transaction descriptions.

- [x] Support full and partial payments with payment date, method, reference and remaining-balance calculation. Reject future-dated settled payments; a nonzero amount already paid during invoice creation requires an explicit actual settlement date and payment method.
